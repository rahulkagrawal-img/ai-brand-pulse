# AI Brand Pulse — Project Brief

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 12 September 2026  
**Status:** Concierge MVP Validation / Pre-SaaS

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**AI Brand Pulse** is a productized **AI Commerce Visibility** platform for Indian D2C, heritage, and export brands.

It diagnoses how well a brand's ecommerce website can be **understood, discovered, and represented by search engines and AI-driven discovery systems**, converts that diagnosis into an evidence-backed **₹2,499 AI Brand Pulse Blueprint**, and—if validation proves demand—will evolve into an automated ongoing visibility monitoring platform.

The business model is deliberately staged:

1. **Concierge MVP** → validate the problem and willingness to pay.
2. **Productized Blueprint** → establish a repeatable paid deliverable.
3. **Automated audit SaaS** → automate evidence collection, scoring, analysis, and delivery.
4. **Ongoing monitoring** → create recurring value and subscription revenue.
5. **Consulting funnel** → surface qualified brands that need higher-value strategic implementation.

The current priority is **validation, not SaaS development**.

# 2. Product Promise

> **Find out how visible and understandable your ecommerce brand is to Google and AI systems — and exactly what to fix.**

The product should turn a vague question—

> "Can customers and AI systems find and understand my brand?"

—into an evidence-backed diagnosis and prioritized action plan.

# 3. Target Customer

Primary customer segments:

### Indian D2C brands
Brands selling directly through their own ecommerce websites.

### Heritage brands
Indian brands with cultural, craft, manufacturing, geographic, or historical identity that can benefit from stronger entity and topical visibility.

### Export brands
Indian brands selling internationally where search visibility, product interpretation, trust, and structured ecommerce information matter.

### Ideal early customer characteristics

Prefer brands with:

- A functioning ecommerce website
- Meaningful product/catalogue depth
- Existing organic-search ambitions
- Founder/marketing decision-maker accessible
- Sufficient commercial intent to invest in visibility
- A clear brand/entity story
- Potential for ongoing SEO/GEO/AI-commerce work

Avoid spending early validation effort on businesses with no meaningful ecommerce presence or no realistic ability/willingness to act on recommendations.

---

# 4. The Problem

Traditional SEO reporting often tells a brand:

- rankings
- traffic
- technical errors
- backlinks
- keywords

AI-driven discovery creates another layer:

- Can systems understand what the brand sells?
- Are products described with enough structured information?
- Is the brand/entity clearly defined?
- Can important facts be reliably extracted?
- Does the website provide answerable content?
- Are product, trust, shipping, returns, and other commercial facts machine-readable?
- Is the website structured so that search and AI systems can interpret it consistently?

AI Brand Pulse addresses this broader **commerce visibility and answerability problem**.

---

# 5. Important Positioning Constraint

AI Brand Pulse must **not falsely claim to measure actual ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, or other AI citations/rankings unless those observations are genuinely measured using a defined data source or test protocol.**

The initial product measures **AI-search / AI-commerce readiness and discoverability signals** from observable evidence.

This distinction is fundamental:

> **Measure what we can actually observe. Do not turn a proxy into a claimed ranking measurement.**

# 6. Current Phase: Concierge MVP

We are intentionally **not building the full SaaS yet**.

The Concierge MVP is designed to answer:

1. Do qualified brands recognize the problem?
2. Do they accept the free audit?
3. Does the audit create meaningful problem resonance?
4. Will they pay ₹2,499 for the Blueprint?
5. Are they satisfied with the Blueprint?
6. Can audit production become significantly faster with tooling?
7. Is there genuine interest in ongoing monitoring?
8. Does the opportunity justify building software?

The human remains in the loop.

The Concierge process is not disposable work. It is the research and specification phase for the eventual software.

# 9. Five-Dimension Audit Framework

The initial scoring model contains five dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Technical SEO | 20% |
| Content & Topical Authority | 25% |
| Entity & Trust | 20% |
| Product / AI Shopping Readiness | 20% |
| AI Discoverability Readiness | 15% |

Overall score:

**Overall = Technical × 0.20 + Content × 0.25 + Entity × 0.20 + Product × 0.20 + AI Discoverability × 0.15**

The detailed scoring rules must be explicit and testable.

# 11. Evidence → Signal → Score → Finding → Recommendation

This is the core product architecture.

## Raw evidence

What the website actually exposes.

Example:

- JSON-LD contains Product schema
- Product page contains price
- No visible returns policy detected

## Derived signal

A deterministic interpretation of the evidence.

Example:

- Product price = detected
- Returns policy = not detected

## Score

A numerical result generated from documented rules.

## Finding

A human-readable interpretation of the scored evidence.

## Recommendation

The action proposed because of the finding.

## AI interpretation

An LLM may explain and prioritize evidence, but it must work only from supplied evidence and derived signals.

This separation must be maintained throughout the product.

---

# 12. Evidence Integrity Rules

AI Brand Pulse must never fabricate:

- Search rankings
- AI citations
- Backlinks
- Reviews
- Certifications
- Product information
- Company information
- Competitor information
- Search visibility
- Third-party mentions

If evidence cannot be established:

> **Not detected**

Do not convert absence of evidence into a factual claim.

Every important recommendation should be traceable to evidence.

---

# 13. Deterministic Scoring Principle

The scoring engine must be deterministic.

Given identical structured evidence:

> **The same input must always produce the same score.**

LLMs must not directly determine numerical scores.

The intended architecture is:

```text
Website evidence
       ↓
Structured signals
       ↓
Deterministic scoring
       ↓
Category scores
       ↓
Overall score
       ↓
AI interpretation
       ↓
Findings + recommendations
```

This makes the product auditable, testable, and defensible.

# 18. Security Principles

Any future URL-fetching system must account for:

- SSRF
- private/local IP access
- malicious redirects
- URL validation
- resource limits
- crawl depth
- response size limits
- timeouts
- hostile HTML/content
- abuse prevention

Never allow arbitrary user-supplied URLs to become an unrestricted server-side network access mechanism.

Security requirements should be designed before production crawling is implemented.

---

# 19. AI Usage Rules

AI is valuable for:

- interpreting structured evidence
- explaining findings
- prioritizing recommendations
- drafting concise founder-facing language
- identifying patterns across evidence
- generating implementation guidance

AI is not authoritative for:

- inventing evidence
- determining unsupported rankings
- claiming AI citations that were not observed
- overriding deterministic scoring
- filling missing facts with plausible guesses

The system should preserve a clear boundary between:

**what was observed**

and

**what the AI inferred or recommended.**

# 21. Build Decision Gate

After Concierge validation:

## BUILD

Proceed toward SaaS if evidence indicates:

- Blueprint conversion ≥20%
- Mean satisfaction ≥8/10
- Audit production time trending down
- Strong evidence of recurring monitoring demand

## PIVOT

If:

- Conversion is roughly 5–20%
- Or satisfaction is roughly 6–8/10

Change one major variable and run another validation batch.

Possible variables:

- Offer
- Price
- Report format
- Positioning
- Target segment
- Blueprint scope

## HOLD

If:

- Conversion <5%
- Or satisfaction <6/10

Do not force the SaaS thesis.

Consider keeping AI Brand Pulse as:

- A consulting diagnostic
- A lead-generation product
- A paid audit/Blueprint service
- A component of a broader consulting offer

# 22. Development Principles

1. **Validation before automation.**
2. **Evidence before interpretation.**
3. **Deterministic measurement before AI judgment.**
4. **Simple architecture before abstraction.**
5. **Test scoring independently of the internet and LLMs.**
6. **Never fabricate evidence.**
7. **Make uncertainty explicit.**
8. **Keep human review where it creates value.**
9. **Build reusable components that can survive into the SaaS.**
10. **Do not build infrastructure simply because it may be needed later.**
11. **Prefer observable, defensible metrics over impressive-sounding claims.**
12. **Every major architectural decision should have a reason.**
